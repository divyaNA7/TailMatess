How It Works

Your Journey to a New Best Friend

We've simplified the adoption process into four easy steps. From browsing to bringing your pet home, we're with you every step of the way.

01

Browse Pets

Explore hundreds of verified pets from trusted shelters across India. Filter by breed, age, location, and temperament.

02

AI Matching

Use our PawMate AI to find the perfect companion based on your lifestyle, home size, and personality preferences.

03

Meet & Bond

Schedule a virtual or in-person meeting with your potential new family member. Build that special connection.

04

Bring Home

Complete the adoption process with our guided paperwork and safe transport to bring your pet home.
